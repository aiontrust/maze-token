About

MAZE ($MAZE) is an ERC-20 token on the Ethereum mainnet, created by an artist and entrepreneur in June 2021 as a working example of how a cryptocurrency can fit into an art business. It is a small, early-stage project built around a simple idea: give a token a real use before talking about its price.

Artists and creators often hear about NFTs and crypto payments without ever seeing them work. MAZE is meant to be something they can touch: accept it, hand it out, and see how it moves.

What MAZE is for

  • Tips and rewards. Thank someone for their work, or reward people for finishing puzzles, mazes and games.
  • Art and merch. Hand out MAZE with artwork, or let people exchange it for merch and for ETH, the main currency of the NFT world.
  • Real-world events. The original idea is a puzzle-style rally through city streets, with checkpoints scanned by QR code, incentives for participants and rewards for finishing the run.
  • Teaching tool. A hands-on way to explain crypto payments and NFTs to artists and small creative businesses.

These are plans and ideas for a project in its early stages. Nothing here is a promise of a product, a return or a price.

Play the games

Three free browser games are built around MAZE.

  • MAZE DINO – Puzzle Adventures. A maze game starring the pink dino. Connect a wallet if you want to tip with MAZE.
  • Dino Daily. A new sliding-tile puzzle every day, plus a mix-and-match dino builder. Points are kept in your browser; they are not MAZE tokens.
  • MAZE TALES. An ad-lib story game: fill in the blanks, reveal a silly dino tale, and share it. It uses the same points and day streak as Dino Daily.

Where the project stands

MAZE is at an early stage. It has not yet been used to pay for anything or to mint NFTs. The creator's NFT demos on OpenSea and Rarible are separate examples of how NFTs work, and they were not bought with or minted using MAZE.

Next: CoinTipsApp, a project to make tipping and paying with MAZE part of a real app.

Transparency

  • The full supply was minted once, when the contract was deployed in June 2021.
  • The contract has no owner functions, no fees on transfers, and no way to block buying or selling. Holders can burn their own tokens.
  • The source code is public and verified on Etherscan, so you can read it yourself.
  • Liquidity is currently small. Large trades will move the price a lot.
